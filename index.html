Welcome to TM Bowl!

Here's how it works
It's a meditation timer intended for the Twenty Minutes Meditation technique. www.twentyminutesmeditation.co.uk
Three stages
A session moves through three stages — Settle in → Meditation → Return. Just tap play to begin.
Settle in
Settle in lets you settle down before you start meditating. The bowl rings at the start of this stage so you can be sure the phone isn't on silent or the volume so low you can't hear it. Want to start in silence instead? Set Settle in to 0 seconds.
Meditation
The bowl rings after Settle in are your cue that the meditation itself has begun.
Return
This is the part where you've finished meditating, but keep your eyes closed for a few minutes while you readjust. The bowl rings are your cue that you're done.
Making changes
Tap the pencil to set times, strikes, the bowl sound, and the starting bell.
